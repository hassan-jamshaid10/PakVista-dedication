Karachi — City of Lights

Pakistan’s largest metropolis, where the Arabian Sea meets a vibrant urban skyline.

About Karachi

Karachi is Pakistan’s largest city and its economic heartbeat, sitting along the Arabian Sea coast in the south of the country. Known as the “City of Lights” for its round-the-clock energy, Karachi blends a bustling port economy with a rich colonial and maritime history. From its sandy beaches to its grand historic landmarks, the city offers visitors a unique mix of culture, commerce, and coastline.

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is Karachi’s most popular seaside destination, stretching along the Arabian Sea coastline. Locals and tourists alike gather here for camel and horse rides, beachside food stalls, and stunning sunset views over the water. It’s the perfect spot to experience the relaxed, coastal side of a city otherwise known for its fast pace.

Mohatta Palace

Built in 1927 as a summer residence, Mohatta Palace is one of Karachi’s most striking architectural landmarks, constructed from pink Jodhpur stone in a blend of Rajasthani and Mughal styles. Today it functions as a museum and cultural center, hosting art exhibitions and preserving artifacts that reflect the region’s diverse heritage.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum showcases the country’s naval history through decommissioned ships, submarines, and aircraft on display. Visitors can step aboard real naval vessels and explore exhibits detailing Pakistan’s maritime defense and seafaring heritage, making it a fascinating stop for history enthusiasts of all ages.
